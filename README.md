AviaSAFEsms bridges traditional regulatory compliance with modern **Human & Organizational Performance (HOP)** and **Safety-II** operational insights.

### ✨ Key Features & Capabilities

* **Integrated SDCPS Reporting:** Multi-source hazard and occurrence reporting workflows designed around ICAO Annex 19 recommendations.
* **Intelligent NLP Report Analysis:** Built-in natural language processing to assist safety managers with automated categorization, human factor identification, and safety trend discovery.
* **Dynamic Risk & Safety Dashboards:** Real-time visibility into risk exposure, open safety actions, and hazard severity matrices.
* **Lightweight & High Availability:** High-speed cloud-native delivery secured by Cloudflare edge caching and Firebase infrastructure.
* **Human Factors & Operational Focus:** Root-cause workflows structured to move beyond simple compliance into proactive system resilience.

---

### 🛠️ Architecture & Technology Stack

* **Frontend:** Multi-page web architecture (HTML5 / CSS3 / Modern JavaScript)
* **Backend & Analytics:** Python / Flask micro-services with integrated NLP analysis fallbacks
* **Edge & Hosting:** Firebase Hosting with Cloudflare DNS & SSL management
* **Data Layer:** Cloud-native state and secure event ingestion
