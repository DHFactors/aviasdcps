📖 Onboarding Guide

ICAO Annex 19 compliant onboarding for State Safety Oversight.

1. Configure Tenant

Select your State/Operator tenant in the header. All data is isolated per tenant.

2. Report Hazards

Use Hazards → New Hazard. Severity and probability map to the risk matrix.

3. State Oversight

Monitor HRC Rate, SPT Score and ALoSP in State Oversight. Charts are tenant-scoped.

4. Audit & Reports

Export audit CSV, generate State Oversight PDF, and trigger scheduled dispatch.
