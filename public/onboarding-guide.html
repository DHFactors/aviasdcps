📖 Evaluator & Onboarding Guide

Structured walkthrough of the Three-Tier Architecture, 4 Core Registers & CAAN CAR 19 Compliance

🚀 Enter AE Portal →

1. Multi-Tenant Scope Selection

Use the top-right VIEWING: dropdown to switch between All Fleets (Aggregated State View) and individual operator tenants (Pacific Air Services, Nordic Aviation, Southern Hemisphere). Data isolation is strictly maintained across all modules.

2. Phase 1 SMS Maturity Assessment

Navigate to 🛡️ SMS Maturity to review the ICAO 4-Pillar / 12-Element scorecard. Ratings (1–5) automatically roll up to calculate the organizational SMS Health Index and provide an auditable quarterly trend.

3. Four Dedicated Safety Registers

Explore the decoupled registers: 📋 Hazards (Intake & VIRS), ⚡ Risks (7 Impact Area Matrix & Bow-Tie), 📋 CANs (Deficiency Notices), and 🛠️ CAPs (Department Action Plans & CAAN App. 6/7 Forms).

4. State Oversight & ALoSP Baselines

Open 🏛️ State View to examine national benchmarking: HRC reporting culture rates, SPT 30-day resolution percentages, ALoSP risk indices, and multi-operator variance analysis under CAAN CAR 19.
